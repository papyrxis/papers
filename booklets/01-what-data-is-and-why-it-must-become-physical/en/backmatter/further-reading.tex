\chapter*{Further Reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Further Reading}
\markboth{Further Reading}{Further Reading}

The works in this section are suggestions for readers who want to explore selected topics in greater depth. This list does not replace the sources cited in the text; the works supporting the book's arguments are listed in the References section.

\section*{Information Theory}
\begin{itemize}
  \item For a more comprehensive mathematical treatment of entropy, coding, and channel capacity, \fullcite{cover2006} is a strong next step.
  \item For a more accessible account of symbols, signals, and noise, \fullcite{pierce1980} is a useful choice.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Algorithmic Complexity}
\begin{itemize}
  \item For a systematic treatment of Kolmogorov complexity and its applications, see \fullcite{livitanyi2008}.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Signals and Physical Computation}
\begin{itemize}
  \item For the mathematics of discrete-time signal processing, \fullcite{oppenheim2010} is a more specialized reference.
  \item For the relationship between thermodynamics and computation, \fullcite{bennett1982} offers a useful review.
\end{itemize}
