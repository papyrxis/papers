%   BOOKLET_YEAR, BOOKLET_EDITION, BOOKLET_NUMBER, BOOKLET_ISBN
%   PDF_TITLE, PDF_AUTHOR, PDF_SUBJECT, PDF_KEYWORDS
% ============================================================
