If you use or refer to this booklet in your own work, the following
citation formats are suggested.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Chicago (author-date):}

\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.5em
\BookletAuthor. \BookletYear. \textit{\BookletTitle.}
Booklet No.\,\BookletNumber\ in \textit{Arliz: Zero to Bit.}
\BookletEdition.
\url{https://github.com/papyrxis/arliz}.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{APA:}

\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.5em
\BookletAuthor. (\BookletYear).
\textit{\BookletTitle}
(Arliz Booklet No.\,\BookletNumber, \BookletEdition).
Retrieved from \url{https://github.com/papyrxis/arliz}

\bigskip
