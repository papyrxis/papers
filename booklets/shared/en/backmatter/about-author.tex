\markboth{About the Author}{About the Author}

\textbf{\BookletAuthor} (\BookletAuthorHandle) is an Iranian
developer and writer with interests in hardware engineering,
low-level programming, and technical writing in Persian and English.

\textit{Arliz: Zero to Bit} is his ongoing attempt to write the
book about computers he wished had existed when he started ---
one that traces a complete, unbroken path from the physics of
a transistor to the data structures used in everyday software,
without hiding the steps between.
