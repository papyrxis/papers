\textit{Arliz: Zero to Bit} is a structured technical book project
that starts from a voltage difference across a transistor and builds
upward --- through binary encoding, integers, floating-point numbers,
characters, pointers, and serialisation --- toward the data structures
and systems that modern software depends on.

The series is organised as self-contained booklets, each covering one
stage in that journey. This booklet --- No.\,\BookletNumber\ in the
series --- is one of approximately fifty planned stages, each of which
can be read independently or in sequence.

The project name comes from the Farsi word for ``array,'' reflecting
the ambition to give a complete, ground-up account of how structured
data is represented, stored, and processed, making each layer of
abstraction visible rather than assumed.

Full source, build tooling, issue tracker, and all published booklets
are available at:

\begin{center}
  \url{https://github.com/papyrxis/arliz}
\end{center}

Readers who spot errors, have questions, or want to follow development
are welcome to open an issue or watch the repository.
