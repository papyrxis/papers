\textit{Arliz} is a project about what data and computation actually
are, followed from the array down to the transistor and back up
through memory, processors, and computation itself. It's written as
three volumes and released as open source: the text under
CC~BY-SA~4.0, the source and build tooling under the MIT License.

This booklet is a self-contained excerpt of \BookletSubtitle, and can
be read on its own. The complete text, including the volumes this
booklet was drawn from, is available at:

\begin{center}
  \url{https://github.com/papyrxis/arliz}
\end{center}

Issues and pull requests on that repository are welcome --- the book
improves through the people who point out what's wrong, unclear, or
missing.
