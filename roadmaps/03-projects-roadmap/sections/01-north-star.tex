\vspace{0.4cm}
\begin{northstarbox}
\textbf{\color{arlizblue}\large $\bigstar$\enspace PROJECTS NORTH STAR}

\vspace{6pt}
Turn a pile of salvaged boards --- a CRT/TV power-and-tuner board, a 6-line telecom
SLIC/PBX card, and three phone motherboards (Huawei + 2$\times$ Nokia) --- into a
\textbf{working home telecom and automation lab}, built from absolute zero knowledge
of electronics, soldering, or networking. Every track below assumes you know nothing
at the start and ends with a physically working, testable device.

\vspace{8pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{\hspace{4pt}}ll}
  \textbf{\color{arlizblue}$\to$} & Track 0\phantom{00} :\enspace Common foundations --- safety, tools, electronics, code, networking \\
  \textbf{\color{arlizblue}$\to$} & Track 1\phantom{00} :\enspace Relay automation panel --- control real appliances from a phone \\
  \textbf{\color{arlizblue}$\to$} & Track 2\phantom{00} :\enspace ESP32 WiFi intercom --- salvaged mic/speaker, real-time audio \\
  \textbf{\color{arlizblue}$\to$} & Track 3\phantom{00} :\enspace PBX/SLIC board --- revive 6-line analog telephony hardware \\
  \textbf{\color{arlizblue}$\to$} & Track 4\phantom{00} :\enspace Analog-to-VoIP gateway --- old rotary phones talk over the internet \\
  \textbf{\color{arlizblue}$\to$} & Track 5\phantom{00} :\enspace Multi-house RF bridge --- connect two homes with antennas + LoRa backup \\
\end{tabular}

\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{\color{arlizgold}First working build target: end of Track 1 (Week 5).}
Each track produces a standalone, demonstrable device. Tracks are ordered by rising
difficulty: mechanical/electrical $\to$ embedded audio $\to$ analog telephony $\to$
VoIP/software $\to$ RF. Skipping ahead is possible, but each track leans on skills
built in the one before it.
\end{northstarbox}
